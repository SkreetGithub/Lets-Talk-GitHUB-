Mandarin Chinese has more native speakers than any other language. Let Talk helps with supplier calls, grandparents and in-laws, and students far from home.

Each person sets their own language in Settings. Let Talk handles the translation both ways, so neither of you has to switch languages.

Questions

Can I translate a phone call from Chinese to English?

Yes. Let Talk translates live voice and video calls between Chinese and English on iPhone. You hear English, they hear Chinese, and subtitles show on screen.

Does it work from English to Chinese too?

Yes. Translation works both ways. Each person sets their own language, and Let Talk translates in both directions.

Does the other person need Let Talk?

Yes. Translated calls and messages work between two Let Talk accounts, so the person you're talking to installs Let Talk too. You can add each other by phone number or by scanning a QR code.
