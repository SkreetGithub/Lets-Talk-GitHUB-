01 · Messages

Text in your language. They read it in theirs.

Every message arrives translated, with the original one tap away. Read receipts show when it's been seen.

02 · Voice calls

Call home, even when home speaks another language.

Speak normally. The other person hears your words translated, and you hear theirs.

03 · Video calls

Face to face, with live subtitles.

See each other and follow along with translated subtitles as the conversation happens.

04 · QR add

Meet someone. Scan. Start talking.

Show your QR code and they're in your contacts in a second. No typing numbers across a language gap.
