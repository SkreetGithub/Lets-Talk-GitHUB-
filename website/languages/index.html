How language settings work

Set your language once in Settings. When you call or message someone, Let Talk translates from your language into theirs and back again. The app also remembers the languages you use most, so they stay at the top of the list.

Questions

How many languages does Let Talk support?

Let Talk's language list has 44 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Polish, Turkish, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Filipino, Hebrew, Ukrainian, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Slovak, Slovenian, Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Greek, Icelandic, Irish, Welsh, Maltese, Basque, Catalan, Galician.

Can two people use different languages on the same call?

Yes. That's the point of Let Talk. Each person speaks and hears their own language.

Does the other person need Let Talk?

Yes. Translated calls and messages work between two Let Talk accounts, so the person you're talking to installs Let Talk too. You can add each other by phone number or by scanning a QR code.

How much does Let Talk cost?

Let Talk is a one-time purchase on the App Store. There is no subscription and no per-minute charge for calls or translation.
