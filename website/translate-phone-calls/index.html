How real-time call translation works

1

You speak normally

No push-to-talk and no handing your phone back and forth. Just talk the way you would on any call.

2

Let Talk translates it

Your speech is turned into text, translated into the other person's language and shown as subtitles.

3

They hear it in their language

The translation is read aloud in the voice you picked. Their reply comes back to you the same way.

A real conversation, not a phrasebook

Most translator apps make you take turns holding the phone up to someone. Let Talk works inside the call itself, so it feels like a normal phone call with someone who happens to understand you.

You choose how your translated voice sounds, from 8 male and 8 female voices, and you can preview each one in Settings.

Calls are encrypted in transit.

Who uses a phone call translator

  • Families split by language. Grandparents, in-laws and cousins who speak another language.
  • Travelers. Call a host, driver or new friend abroad without a phrasebook.
  • Small businesses. Talk with suppliers, clients and crews in their own language.
  • Couples. Date across a language gap and still say exactly what you mean.

Questions

Can an app translate a phone call in real time?

Yes. Let Talk translates voice calls live on iPhone. Each person speaks their own language and hears the other person in theirs, with subtitles on screen.

Does the other person need Let Talk?

Yes. Translated calls and messages work between two Let Talk accounts, so the person you're talking to installs Let Talk too. You can add each other by phone number or by scanning a QR code.

How much does Let Talk cost?

Let Talk is a one-time purchase on the App Store. There is no subscription and no per-minute charge for calls or translation.

Is Let Talk available on Android?

Let Talk is available for iPhone today.

How many languages can Let Talk translate?

Let Talk's language list has 44 languages, including Spanish, French, Chinese, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Hindi and Russian. See the full list.

Can I choose the voice the other person hears?

Yes. Go to Settings → Voice Settings to preview and pick from 8 male and 8 female voices.
